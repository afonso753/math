$\mathbf{20.}$ \textbf{Let} $\mathbf{d=(826,1890).}$ \textbf{Use the Euclidean algorithm to compute} $\mathbf{d,}$ \textbf{then express} $\mathbf{d}$ \textbf{as a linear combination of} $\mathbf{826}$ \textbf{and} $\mathbf{1890.}$ 

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{align*}
    1890 &= 826 \times 2 + 238 \\
    826  &= 238 \times 3 + 112 \\
    238  &= 112 \times 2 + 14 \\
    112  &= 14 \times 8 + 0,
\end{align*}

then $d=14.$ Furthermore, using the equations above we conclude that,

\begin{align*}
    14=7 \times 1890 -16 \times 826.
\end{align*}
