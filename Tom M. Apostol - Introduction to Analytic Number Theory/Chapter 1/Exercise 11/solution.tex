\textbf{Prove that} $\mathbf{n^4+4}$ \textbf{is composite if} $n>1.$

\vspace{0.5cm}
For $n>1$, $n^4+4$ can be decomposed in two factors (both bigger than one) in the following way:
\begin{align*}
    n^4+4 &= (n^2+2)^2-4n^2\\
          &= (n^2+2)^2-(2n)^2\\
          &= (n^2+2-2n)(n^2+2+2n)\\
          &>1\times 5
\end{align*}
